Code of Conduct
===============

Our community is dedicated to providing an inclusive, harassment-free experience for everyone.

Expected behavior
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful and considerate of others' viewpoints and experiences
- Be constructive and accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, hateful or discriminatory language, or violent threats
- Personal insults, sustained disruption, or targeting individuals
- Sharing private information without consent

Enforcement
Reports will be reviewed by project maintainers and may result in a warning, removal of offending content, temporary or permanent bans, or other actions as appropriate.

Reporting
To report unacceptable behavior, please open a private issue or contact the maintainers (or email conduct@example.com) with: project name, date/time, people involved, description, and any evidence.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issue trackers, chat, mailing lists, and public events).
